Mountains I've Explored

⛰️

Mount Merbabu

Savanna landscapes, long ridge trails, and panoramic summit views. Ideal for endurance training and open-terrain navigation.

🌋

Mount Sindoro

Steep ascents with volcanic sand and open slopes. Demands strong pacing strategy and physical stamina.

🏔️

Mount Sumbing

Technical and challenging routes with long elevation gain. Suitable for advanced hikers and mental resilience training.

🌄

Mount Prau

Friendly trails for beginners with beautiful sunrise spots. Perfect for introduction hikes and beginner guidance programs.

🌿

Mount Lawu

Cultural and historical mountain with diverse ecosystems. Long-distance trekking with steady elevation and spiritual atmosphere.

🌅

Mount Batur

A popular sunrise trekking destination with volcanic terrain and a scenic caldera lake view. Suitable for beginner to intermediate hikers.
